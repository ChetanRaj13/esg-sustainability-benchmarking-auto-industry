# Comparative Sustainability Benchmarking of Passenger Vehicle Manufacturers in India

## 📊 A BRSR-Based ESG Analysis

**Companies:** Tata Motors, Maruti Suzuki, Mahindra & Mahindra

---

## 📌 Overview

This project presents a **comparative ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance) analysis** of leading Indian passenger vehicle manufacturers using the **Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting (BRSR)** framework mandated by SEBI.

## 🎯 Objective

* Benchmark sustainability performance across companies in the same NIC category
* Develop **data-driven ESG indicators aligned with BRSR Core Attributes**
* Construct **justified benchmarks (Good / Acceptable / Poor)**
* Provide **strategic insights and recommendations**

---

## 🏭 Companies Analysed

* Tata Motors Limited
* Maruti Suzuki India Limited
* Mahindra & Mahindra Limited

---

## ⚙️ Methodology

The analysis strictly uses **public BRSR disclosures** as the primary data source.

### Key Steps:

* Data extraction from BRSR reports
* Indicator construction (intensity + percentage metrics)
* Benchmark development using:

  * Policy frameworks (BEE, SEBI, NDC)
  * ESG literature
* Use of **proxy indicators** where data gaps exist
* Scoring framework:

  ```
  Good = 3 | Acceptable = 2 | Poor = 1
  ```

As described in the report (Methodology section), the framework ensures:

* Comparability
* Transparency
* Policy alignment 

---

## 📈 Key ESG Indicators

### 🌱 Environmental

* Energy Intensity (GJ/₹ crore)
* Renewable Energy Share (%)
* GHG Emissions Intensity
* Water Intensity
* Water Recycling (Proxy)
* Hazardous Waste Share (Proxy)

### 👥 Social

* Gender Diversity (% women workforce)
* Training Coverage (Proxy)
* Workplace Safety (LTIFR)

### 🏛 Governance

* Board Gender Diversity
* Supplier ESG Oversight (Proxy)

---

## 📊 Key Findings

### 🥇 Leader: Tata Motors

* Strongest performance across **energy, emissions, and governance**
* Best **renewable energy adoption (~30%)**
* Lowest **GHG intensity (0.65 tCO₂e/₹ cr)** 

### ⚖️ Transition Player: Mahindra & Mahindra

* Strong **water recycling (39%)**
* Improving ESG transparency
* Moderate overall performance

### ⚠️ Laggard: Maruti Suzuki

* Highest **energy intensity (37.69 GJ/₹ cr)**
* Lowest renewable share (~6%)
* Weak governance diversity

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Sustainability leadership is driven by **efficiency and governance**, not scale
* **Intensity-based metrics** are more useful than absolute values
* **Gender diversity is a sector-wide issue**
* ESG integration varies significantly across firms

---

## 📌 Strategic Recommendations

### For Companies:

* Improve **renewable energy adoption**
* Strengthen **board diversity**
* Embed ESG into **operational KPIs**

### For Sector:

* Standardise BRSR disclosures
* Promote **circular economy practices**
* Improve **female workforce participation**

---

## ⚠️ Limitations

* Reliance on **public BRSR data only**
* Use of **proxy indicators** due to data gaps
* Differences in reporting boundaries across firms

These limitations are explicitly addressed in the report to maintain transparency
